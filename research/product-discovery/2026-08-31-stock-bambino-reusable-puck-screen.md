# Product discovery: reusable puck screen for the stock Bambino basket

- Run date: 2026-08-31
- Guide/cluster: `/guides/budget-home-espresso-setup/` / compact espresso
- Reader decision: Is any reusable puck screen worth the added headspace, hot-disc removal, and cleaning step for a stock 54mm Breville Bambino or Bambino Plus basket when the recommendation is limited to workflow and maintenance rather than promised extraction results?
- Experience basis: Research-only. Coffee Rigs did not physically test any screen, basket, dose, cleaning method, or extraction result.
- Why this problem is unresolved: The live guide now covers the machine, scale, dosing funnel, WDT tool, and tamper but does not tell a stock-basket owner whether a reusable screen reduces more cleaning burden than it creates. The prior discovery note identified this exact gap.
- Evidence limitations: Basket taper and coffee volume determine headspace, so a nominal 53.3mm or 53.35mm diameter does not prove that a screen will sit flat above a given dose. Owner reports are mixed and are not failure rates. Manufacturer extraction, channeling, flavor, durability, food-grade, and machine-longevity language was not adopted as independent fact. A current Amazon page with no featured offer is a listing identity check, not a purchasable recommendation. The CPSC recall CSV no-match is a dated database result, not safety certification.
