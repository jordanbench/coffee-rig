Breville Bambino vs Bambino Plus

Choose between manual milk control and hands-free texturing without mistaking the longer feature list for a universal upgrade.

The short answer

Choose the regular Bambino when you want the smaller manual-milk workflow and are willing to learn steam-wand technique. Choose the Bambino Plus only when your household makes milk drinks often enough for automatic temperature and texture control to remove a recurring burden. Neither machine includes a grinder, neither can brew and steam at the same time, and neither turns a poor grinder into a consistent espresso setup.

This is a research-only comparison. Coffee Rigs did not physically test either machine. It does not publish prices or availability; confirm the exact model, included accessories, seller, return terms, and current listing details on Amazon before buying.

Choose by the routine you repeat

Mostly straight espresso or Americanos

Start with the regular Bambino. The Plus's defining workflow advantage is automatic milk, so a household that rarely steams milk would be carrying extra complexity without solving its main coffee problem.

One or more milk drinks every day

Consider the Bambino Plus when hands-free texturing would meaningfully reduce morning attention. The deciding question is not whether automation exists; it is whether you will use it often enough to accept its purge, drip-tray, sensor, and cleaning routine.

You want to learn manual steaming

Choose the regular Bambino. Breville's current support hub includes a manual texturing workflow, while the Plus support hub documents automatic texturing. That is a workflow difference, not a claim that one method makes objectively better milk.

You do not own an espresso grinder

Pause the machine decision and solve the grinder first. Both machines can be limited by inconsistent grind size and dose. A capable grinder is part of the setup, not an optional future fix for a machine-only purchase.
